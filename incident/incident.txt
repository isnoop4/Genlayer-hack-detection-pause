SECURITY ALERT: TestSwap exploit in progress

At 14:02 UTC attackers began draining TestSwap liquidity pools using a flash-loan
price manipulation. Roughly 4.2 million dollars in tokens have already left the pools
and the drain is still going on. The TestSwap team confirmed the attack and says it is
investigating. Users are advised to withdraw liquidity and avoid new deposits until
the team publishes an all-clear.
